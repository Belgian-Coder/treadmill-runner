## Full description

TreadmillRunner Companion provides a simple watch-first recording flow for household treadmill runs.

Press Select to begin a native Garmin treadmill activity. Press Select again to stop and save it. The completed activity follows the watch's normal Garmin Connect synchronization, so the watch remains the authoritative activity recorder.

Optionally pair the app with a local TreadmillRunner gateway to show the selected runner, planned session, and connection state. The paired connection is informational. This app never starts, stops, pauses, or changes the speed or incline of a treadmill. Physical treadmill controls and the safety key remain authoritative.

The app still records in Standalone mode when the phone, home gateway, or network is unavailable.
